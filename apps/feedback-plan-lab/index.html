    What the added sources changed
    The lifecycle framework asks for a boundary, stimulus, response and value. The design-process sources ask what becomes stale and what must be repeated. Together they make a feedback experiment more useful here than starting with a reusable module library.
    This construction uses ordinary token rules and finite observed-state planning. It adapts selected source methods; it does not implement the full ASM2.0 simulator or claim a general categorical theory of feedback.
