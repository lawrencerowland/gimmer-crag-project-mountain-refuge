## Useful differences

- One owned lifting resource permits two serial task orders but forbids their joint start. Two independent resources permit that joint start; the representative toy schedules finish at 5 and 3 time units respectively.
- A connected receiver's former external supply is discharged. Keeping it fabricates another resource.
- The selective N connection retains five complete orders and permits C before unrelated B finishes. A whole-fragment barrier loses that freedom.
- Either producer may supply C: all four lawful selected-work orders survive. Taking all producer edges as requirements wrongly changes either-support to both-support.
- Matching type labels do not grant access across unconnected ownership. A currently blocked method remains represented by its open demand.

These are changed assumptions or lawful planning choices in a fictional refuge model. They do not establish that modular descriptions will save practitioners effort. ASM2.0's documented difficulty with reusable process libraries remains a practical challenge.

## Findings that changed the implementation

Independent challenge caught a comparison that initially checked steps but ignored remaining token counts. A zero-event supply of one and a supply of two exposed the error; full marking comparison is now required. Counts that become too large after legal gluing now report an incomplete calculation consistently, rather than incorrectly turning one route into an invalid model. Ordinary identifiers such as `constructor` no longer collide with JavaScript object properties.

The compiled contract can be composed with its source-arc audit copy removed. Internal state tables and symbolic boundary demands/effects supply the composition route; the original arcs belong to the independently checked source-net route.

Interface review corrected pending resource edits that the main Check button could otherwise discard, mismatched example names after reopening saved work, malformed descriptive metadata, and the empty-scope completion display. Delayed-file and cancelled-worker challenges preserve newer state. Imported observations are informational only: opening a model recomputes the result.
